Tab5 agent usage dashboard: three concepts

Each frame is the device's real size, 1280 by 720. All figures are sample values.

A. Gauge glass

Limits and credit are liquid in measuring tubes: Claude Code limits fill up as you use them, OpenRouter credit drains. The levels glide to new values, and a tube turns red above 90%.

B. Day dial

A 24-hour clock face with midnight at the top. Each hour's spend is a bar pointing outward, the hand marks now, and the two outer rings are the 5-hour and 7-day limits. The current hour's bar grows while you work.

C. Poster

Two flat colour fields and very large numerals, readable from across the room. The numerals count up as spend arrives, and the bottom band is the last 30 days.
